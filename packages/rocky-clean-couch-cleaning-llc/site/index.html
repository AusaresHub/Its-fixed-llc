Common Questions

How long does it take to dry?

Typically 4 to 8 hours. We use high-powered fans to speed up drying time.

Is it safe for kids and pets?

Yes. We use non-toxic, eco-friendly cleaning solutions throughout.

How often should I clean my carpet?

Every 12 to 18 months, or more often with pets or heavy foot traffic.

Do I need to move furniture?

We move light furniture for you. Please move valuables beforehand.

Do you clean area rugs?

Yes. We clean rugs in-place, or offer pickup and return service.
