Good to Know

Before your visit

Do I need an appointment?

Booking ahead is best so we can hold your time and room. You can request a session right here, or call us at (303) 919-2262 — we’ll do our best to fit you in.

What should I expect on my first visit?

We’ll talk through your goals and any problem areas first, then tailor the session to you. Arrive a few minutes early so you can settle in and fully relax.

What do I wear during a massage?

Undress to your comfort level. You’re always professionally draped throughout the session, and only the area being worked on is ever uncovered.

Do you offer couples massage?

Yes — our double suite is set up for two side-by-side tables, so you can share a relaxing session with a partner or friend.

What’s your cancellation policy?

Life happens. Just give us a heads-up at least 24 hours ahead and we’ll happily reschedule your appointment.
