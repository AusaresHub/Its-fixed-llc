Good to know

Frequently asked

How do I book a grooming appointment?

Use the booking form on this page, tap the chat button, or call us at (720) 628-9725. Tell us your dog's breed and what they need and we'll get you scheduled.

Do you groom all breeds and sizes?

Yes — from tiny pups to big fluffy guys. We tailor every groom to your dog's coat type, size, and temperament.

My dog gets anxious. Can you still help?

Absolutely. We go slow, stay calm, and keep first visits gentle and positive so nervous dogs feel safe.

How long does a full groom take?

Usually 2 to 4 hours depending on your dog's size and coat. We'll give you a pickup window when you drop off.

How much does grooming cost?

Pricing depends on your dog's breed, size, coat condition, and the services you choose. Give us a call at (720) 628-9725 and we'll give you a quote for your pup.
