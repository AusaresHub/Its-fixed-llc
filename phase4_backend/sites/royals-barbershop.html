Good to Know

Questions

Do you take walk-ins?

Walk-ins are always welcome. Booking ahead just guarantees your spot and lets you request a specific barber.

How do I book a chair?

Use the booking form above, call us, or tap the chat assistant in the corner — it can take your details and book you in seconds.

Do you do beard work and shaves?

Absolutely. Beard shaping, lineups, and classic hot-towel straight-razor shaves are all part of what we do.

How much does a cut cost?

Pricing depends on length, style, and barber, so the figures on this page are starting-point guides. Call (720) 569-7282 to confirm before your visit.

What forms of payment do you take?

Cash and all major cards. Tips are appreciated but never expected.
