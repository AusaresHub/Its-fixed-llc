Good to know

Frequently asked

Do I need an appointment?

Booking ahead is best so we can hold your time and therapist. You can request a session right here, or call us and we'll find a time that works.

What should I expect on my first visit?

We'll start with a quick conversation about your goals and any problem areas, then tailor the session to you. Arrive a few minutes early to settle in.

What do I wear?

Undress to your comfort level. You're always professionally draped throughout the session, with only the area being worked on uncovered.

What's your cancellation policy?

Life happens. Just give us a heads-up at least 24 hours ahead and we'll happily reschedule your appointment.

How much does a session cost?

Pricing varies by modality and session length. Call us or ask the booking assistant for current rates and any package options.
