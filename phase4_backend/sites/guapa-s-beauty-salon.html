Good to Know

Questions, answered

Do you take walk-ins or appointments?

Both! Walk-ins are welcome when we have an open chair, but booking ahead means no wait and time with your favorite tech. Request your spot right here or by phone.

How long does gel or dip last?

With proper care, gel and dip typically stay flawless for two to three weeks without chipping.

Do you do custom nail art?

Absolutely — from simple accents to full custom designs. Bring a photo or your inspiration and we'll bring it to life.

Are your tools sanitized?

Always. Tools are fully sanitized between every client, so you can relax knowing your visit is clean and safe.

How much will my set cost?

Pricing starts at the amounts on our menu and varies with length, shape, and design. We'll always give you an honest quote before we start — no surprises.
