Good to know

Questions, answered

How do I book an appointment?

Use the booking form above, tap the chat button, or call us at (720) 242-9925. Tell us your dog’s breed and what they need and we’ll get you set up.

Do you groom all breeds and sizes?

Yes — from tiny pups to big fluffy guys. We tailor every groom to your dog’s coat type and temperament.

My dog gets anxious. Can you help?

Absolutely. We go slow, keep things calm, and make first visits gentle and positive so your dog learns grooming is nothing to fear.

How long does a full groom take?

Usually 2 to 4 hours depending on size and coat. We’ll give you a pickup window when you drop off.

How much will it cost?

Service prices start at the amounts listed above, but final pricing depends on your dog’s size, coat, and condition. Ask us for an exact quote when you book.
