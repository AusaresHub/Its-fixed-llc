Good to Know

Frequently asked questions

How do I book an appointment?

Use the booking form above, tap the chat button, or call us at (720) 510-9336. Tell us your dog's breed and what they need, and we'll get you set up.

Do you groom all breeds and sizes?

Yes — from tiny pups to big fluffy guys. We tailor every groom to your dog's coat, size and temperament.

My dog gets anxious. Can you help?

Absolutely. We go slow, stay calm and keep first visits gentle and positive. Just let us know ahead of time so we can plan extra patience.

How long does a full groom take?

Usually 2 to 4 hours depending on your dog's size and coat. We'll give you a pickup window when you drop off.

How much does grooming cost?

Pricing starts at the figures listed above and depends on your dog's size, coat and condition. Call (720) 510-9336 for a quick, accurate quote — we're happy to help.
