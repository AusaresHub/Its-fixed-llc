Good to know

Questions, answered

Do you offer both veterinary care and grooming?

Yes. Special Friends is a veterinary clinic and a grooming salon. You can book a wellness exam, vaccines, dental, or a sick visit, plus a full groom, bath, or nail trim — often in the same trip.

How do I book an appointment?

Use the appointment form on this page, chat with our booking assistant, or call us at (303) 363-6636. Let us know your pet's name, type, and what they need, and we'll find a time that works.

Do you see cats as well as dogs?

Absolutely. We care for both dogs and cats and tailor each visit to your pet's needs and temperament.

My pet gets anxious at the vet. Can you help?

Yes. We take a calm, patient approach for nervous, senior, and first-time patients — going slowly and keeping visits as low-stress as possible for both medical care and grooming.

What are your hours?

We're open Monday through Friday from 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM and Saturday from 8:00 AM to 4:00 PM. We're closed Sundays. Hours may vary on holidays, so please call ahead to confirm.

How much do services cost?

Pricing depends on your pet's size, breed, and the specific care they need. Please call us at (303) 363-6636 and we'll be glad to give you a quote.
