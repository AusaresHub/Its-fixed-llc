What we do

Grooming, Tail to Toes

Everything your dog needs to look sharp and feel their best, all in one calm, friendly visit.

Full Groom

Bath, haircut, blow-dry, nails and ears, the whole works. Your pup leaves clean, styled and smelling fantastic.

Bath & Brush

A deep, sudsy clean with conditioner, a thorough brush-out and a nail trim to finish. Fresh and fluffy.

Nail Trim & Grind

Quick, calm and smooth. A tidy trim and grind so paws stay comfortable on every walk. In and out.

De-Shedding Treatment

A deep coat treatment that cuts loose fur way down, so there is less shedding on your couch and in your car.

Puppy's First Groom

A gentle, patient introduction to grooming for young pups, building good habits and a love of the tub early.

Teeth & Ear Care

Add-on freshening for clean ears and better breath, keeping the parts that are easy to forget in great shape.

Good to know

Questions, Answered

How do I book an appointment?

Fill out the booking form on this page or tap the chat button in the corner. Tell us your dog's breed and what they need, and we'll reach out by email or text to confirm a time.

Do you groom all breeds and sizes?

Yes — from tiny pups to big fluffy guys. We tailor every groom to your dog's coat type, breed and temperament so they look and feel their best.

My dog gets anxious. Can you help?

Absolutely. We go slow, stay calm and keep first visits gentle and positive. Just let us know in your booking notes so we can plan extra time and patience.

How long does a full groom take?

Usually two to four hours depending on your dog's size and coat. We'll give you a pickup window when you drop off so you can plan around it.

Where are you located?

We're at 13993 E Utah Cir, Aurora, CO 80012, serving Aurora and the greater Denver metro. Tap "Get Directions" in the Visit section for the map.
