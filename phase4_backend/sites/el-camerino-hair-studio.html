Good to Know

Before you book

Do you take walk-ins or appointments?

El Camerino is a by-appointment studio so you get focused, unhurried time in the chair. Book through the form, the chat, or give us a call — we will find a slot that works.

How much will my service cost?

Pricing depends on your hair length, density, and the look you want, which is why we quote after a quick chat. Call us or ask in the booking chat and we will give you an honest estimate up front.

Do you do color corrections and balayage?

Yes. We offer dimensional color, balayage, gloss, grey blending, and color work. For bigger transformations we recommend a consultation first so we can plan it properly.

Can you do hair for a wedding or event?

Absolutely — updos, soft waves, and special-occasion styling are some of our favorites. Book ahead, especially for weekend dates, so we can reserve your time.

Where are you located?

We are at 18607 Green Valley Ranch Blvd, Suite 100, Denver, CO 80249, serving Denver and the surrounding metro. Tap “Get Directions” for the map.
