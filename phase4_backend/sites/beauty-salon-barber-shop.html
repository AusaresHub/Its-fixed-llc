Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Are you a salon or a barbershop?

Both! We're a combined hair salon and barbershop, so you can get color, cuts, perms and styling alongside fades, beard work and shaves — all in one place.

Do you take walk-ins?

Yes, walk-ins are always welcome. Booking ahead helps guarantee your spot, especially on busy Saturdays.

How much do services cost?

Pricing depends on the service, hair length and what you'd like done. Call us at (720) 643-7863 or ask in the booking chat and we'll give you a quote.

Do you cut kids' hair?

Absolutely. We're a family-friendly shop and happy to take care of kids of all ages.

How do I book an appointment?

Use the booking form above, tap the chat button to book in seconds, or simply give us a call. We'll confirm your time.
