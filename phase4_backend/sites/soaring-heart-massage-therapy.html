Good to Know

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need an appointment?

Yes — booking ahead lets us hold your time and prepare the studio for you. You can request an appointment using the form above, or simply call (720) 205-2320.

What should I expect on my first visit?

We'll talk through your goals and any problem areas first, then tailor the session to you. Plan to arrive a few minutes early so you can settle in and relax.

What should I wear?

Undress to your comfort level — you're always professionally draped throughout the session, and only the area being worked on is uncovered.

How do I choose the right type of massage?

Tell us what's going on — stress, chronic tension, recovery, pregnancy — and we'll recommend the best fit. You can also select "help me choose" on the booking form.

What is your cancellation policy?

Life happens. Just give us at least 24 hours' notice and we'll happily reschedule your session.
