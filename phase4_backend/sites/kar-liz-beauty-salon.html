Good to Know

Questions, answered

Do you take walk-ins or appointments?

Both. Walk-ins are welcome whenever we have an opening, but booking ahead means no wait and time reserved with your favorite tech. You can request a slot right here or call us.

How long does gel or dip last?

With proper care, gel and dip typically wear two to three weeks without chipping. We'll share quick aftercare tips so your set lasts as long as possible.

Do you do custom nail art?

Yes — from subtle accents to full custom designs. Bring a photo or your inspiration and we'll create a look that's all yours.

How are prices set?

The prices listed are starting points. Final pricing depends on length, design complexity, and the condition of your nails or hair, so we'll always confirm before we begin. Give us a call for an exact quote.

Are your tools sanitized?

Always. Tools are fully sanitized between every client. A clean, spotless space is something we take seriously for your safety and comfort.
