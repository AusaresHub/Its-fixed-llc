Good to know

Frequently asked

Do you take walk-ins or appointments?

Both. Walk-ins are welcome whenever a chair is open, but booking ahead means no wait and time with your favorite tech. You can request an appointment right here.

How long does gel or dip powder last?

With proper care, gel and dip typically last two to three weeks without chipping. We’ll always prep your nails properly for the best wear.

Do you do custom nail art?

Yes — from subtle accents and French finishes to full custom designs. Bring a photo or an idea and we’ll bring it to life. Art pricing depends on the design.

Are gift certificates available?

Yes — gift certificates are available in salon. Just ask at the front desk and we’ll set you up.

Where are you located?

We’re at 8400 E Iliff Ave #7, Denver, CO 80231, serving Denver and the surrounding metro. Call (720) 573-8797 with any questions.
