Frequently Asked

Do you take walk-ins?

Yes — walk-ins are always welcome. Booking ahead simply guarantees your spot and the time you want, especially on weekends.

How do I book a chair?

Use the booking form on this page, tap the chat assistant in the corner, or call us at (303) 366-1822. We'll confirm your time by phone.

What does a cut cost?

Pricing starts around $35 for a signature haircut and varies by length, design and detail. Give us a call and we'll quote you for exactly what you're after.

Do you do beard work and shaves?

Absolutely. We offer beard trims and shaping, hot-towel straight-razor shaves, and a cut-and-beard combo for a full reset.

Where are you located?

We're at 9721 E Colfax Ave, Aurora, CO 80010, serving Aurora and the greater Denver metro. Tap "Get directions" for the map.
