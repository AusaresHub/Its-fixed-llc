Good to know

Frequently asked questions

How do I book an appointment?

Use the request form above, tap the chat button in the corner, or call us at (303) 658-0542. Tell us your dog's breed and what they need, and we'll get you on the schedule.

Do you groom all breeds and sizes?

Yes — from tiny pups to big fluffy guys. We tailor every groom to your dog's coat type, breed, and temperament.

My dog gets anxious. Can you help?

Absolutely. We go slow, stay calm, and make first visits gentle and positive. Just let us know what makes your dog nervous so we can plan around it.

How long does a full groom take?

Usually 2 to 4 hours depending on your dog's size and coat. We'll give you a pickup window when you drop off.

How much does grooming cost?

Pricing depends on your dog's size, breed, and coat condition, so we quote each pup individually. Call us or send a request and we'll give you a quick, honest estimate.
