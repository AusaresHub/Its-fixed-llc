Good to Know

Questions, Answered

What is Reiki, and what does a session feel like?

Reiki is a gentle, hands-on (or hands-near) energy-healing practice meant to encourage deep relaxation and balance. Most clients stay fully clothed and describe it as calming and grounding. We'll explain everything before we begin.

Do I need an appointment?

Booking ahead is best so we can hold your time and practitioner. You can request a session using the form above or by calling us — we confirm every booking personally.

What should I expect on my first visit?

We'll talk through your goals and any problem areas, then tailor the session — massage, Reiki, or a blend — to what you need that day.

What do I wear?

For massage, undress to your comfort level; you're always professionally draped throughout. For Reiki, you stay comfortably clothed.

What's your cancellation policy?

Life happens. Just give us a heads-up 24 hours ahead and we'll happily reschedule.
