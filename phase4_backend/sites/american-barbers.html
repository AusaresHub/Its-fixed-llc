Good to Know

Questions, Answered

Do you take walk-ins?

Yes — walk-ins are always welcome. Booking ahead simply guarantees your spot and your time.

How do I book?

Use the booking form on this page, call us at (720) 645-3123, or tap the chat assistant in the corner to book a chair in a few messages.

Do you do beard work and shaves?

Absolutely — beard shaping, line-ups, and classic hot-towel straight-razor shaves are some of our most-requested services.

What does a cut cost?

Pricing starts from the amounts shown in the menu and varies by length, style, and barber. Call us and we'll give you an exact quote.

What payments do you accept?

Cash and all major cards. Tips are appreciated but never expected.
