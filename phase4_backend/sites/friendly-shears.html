Good to know

Questions, answered

Do you take walk-ins?

Always — walk-ins are welcome. Booking a chair ahead just guarantees your spot, especially on weekends.

How do I book a chair?

Fill out the booking form on this page, tap “Book a Chair,” or chat with us using the button in the corner — it books you in a few quick steps.

Do you do beard work and hot-towel shaves?

Yes. We do beard shaping and lineups as well as classic straight-razor, hot-towel shaves.

Are kids welcome?

Absolutely. We’re a friendly, patient shop and love taking care of younger clients (ages 12 and under).

What payment do you accept?

Cash and all major cards. Tips are appreciated but never expected.
