Good to know

Mobile Massage FAQ

What exactly is a cannabis-infused massage?

It pairs a professional massage with topical CBD or cannabis-derived botanical products applied to the skin. They're used purely for relaxation and full-body comfort — you stay in control of your session, and you can always keep it classic with no infused products at all.

Will an infused session make me feel intoxicated?

Our infused offerings are topical and focused on soothing, relaxing bodywork rather than intoxication. We're happy to walk you through the options when you book so you can choose what feels right for you. Infused services are for adults 21+.

What do I need to provide for a mobile appointment?

Just a small amount of space and a quiet spot. We bring the massage table, fresh linens, oils, music, and everything else needed to create a calming treatment space in your home, hotel, or office.

Which areas do you serve?

We provide mobile massage throughout Denver and the surrounding metro. If you're a little outside our standard area, reach out — we'll let you know what's possible.

What should I wear and what can I expect?

Undress to your comfort level — you're professionally draped throughout the entire session. Your therapist will talk through your goals and any problem areas first, then tailor the massage to you.

What's your cancellation policy?

Life happens. Just give us a heads-up at least 24 hours ahead and we'll happily reschedule your appointment.
