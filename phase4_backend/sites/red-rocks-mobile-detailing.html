Frequently asked questions

Do you come to me, or do I come to you?
We come to you. Red Rocks is fully mobile — we arrive at your home, office, or jobsite with our own water, power, and professional products. You don't have to drive anywhere or wait around a shop.
How long does a full detail take?
Most full details run about 3 to 5 hours depending on the size and condition of the vehicle. A wash & wax or interior-only service is quicker. We'll give you a time estimate when we confirm your booking.
How do I book?
Fill out the booking form on this page or use the chat assistant in the corner. Share your address, vehicle, and the service you want, and we'll reply with confirmed pricing and an available time window.
What areas do you serve?
We're based in Denver near W Pacific Pl and serve the city plus the surrounding metro — including Lakewood, Englewood, Wheat Ridge, Aurora, and Centennial. Not sure if you're in range? Send your address in the form and we'll let you know.
How much does a detail cost?
Services start from $60 for an exterior wash & wax, $90 for interior, $199 for a full detail, and $499 for ceramic coating. Final pricing depends on your vehicle's size and condition, which we confirm before any work begins.
