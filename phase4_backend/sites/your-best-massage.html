Good to know

Questions, answered

Do I need an appointment?

Booking ahead is best so we can hold your time and therapist. You can request a time right here, or call us and we'll find your spot.

What should I expect on my first visit?

We'll talk through your goals and any problem areas, then tailor the session to you. Arrive a few minutes early so you can settle in and fully relax.

What do I wear during the massage?

Undress to your comfort level. You're always professionally draped throughout the session, and only the area being worked on is ever exposed.

How do I choose the right massage?

Not sure which to book? Just tell us how you're feeling and what you'd like to work on — we'll recommend the best option and adjust pressure as we go.

What's your cancellation policy?

Life happens. Just give us a heads-up at least 24 hours ahead and we'll happily reschedule you for another time.
