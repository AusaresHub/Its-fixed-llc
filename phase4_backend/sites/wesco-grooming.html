Good to know

Questions, answered

How do I book an appointment?

Fill out the booking form above, message us with the chat button, or call (303) 455-3445. Tell us your dog's breed and what they need and we'll set you up.

Do you groom all breeds and sizes?

Yes — from tiny pups to big, fluffy coats. We tailor every groom to your dog's coat type and temperament.

My dog gets anxious. Can you help?

Absolutely. We keep things calm and unhurried, go slow, and make first visits gentle and positive.

How long does a full groom take?

Usually two to four hours depending on size and coat. We'll give you a pickup window when you drop off.

How much does a groom cost?

Pricing depends on your dog's breed, size, and coat condition. Call or message us for a quick, no-pressure quote.
