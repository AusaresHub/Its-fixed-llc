Good to Know

Frequently asked questions

How do I book?

Use the booking form on this page or call us. Tell us your dog's breed and what they need and we'll get you set up.

Do you groom all breeds and sizes?

Yes — from tiny pups to big fluffy guys. We tailor every groom to your dog's coat and temperament.

My dog gets anxious. Can you help?

Absolutely. We go slow, stay calm and make first visits gentle and positive so your dog feels safe.

How long does a full groom take?

Usually 2 to 4 hours depending on size and coat. We'll give you a pickup window when you drop off.
