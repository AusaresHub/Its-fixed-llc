Good to Know

How do I book?

Just message us right here or call. Tell us your dog's breed and what they need and we'll get you set.

Do you groom all breeds and sizes?

Yes, from tiny pups to big fluffy guys. We tailor the groom to your dog's coat and temperament.

My dog gets anxious. Can you help?

Absolutely. We go slow, stay calm, and make first visits gentle and positive.

How long does a full groom take?

Usually 2 to 4 hours depending on size and coat. We'll give you a pickup window when you drop off.
