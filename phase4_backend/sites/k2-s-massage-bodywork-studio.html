FAQ

Good to know

Do I need an appointment?

Yes — we work by appointment so we can hold your time and therapist. You can request a session through the form above, call us, or book via the chat assistant in seconds.

How is deep tissue different from a regular massage?

Deep tissue uses slower, firmer pressure that reaches the deeper muscle and fascia layers to break up chronic knots. A Swedish session is lighter and more about relaxation. We'll match the pressure to your body and your goals.

What should I expect on my first visit?

We'll talk through your goals and any problem areas first, then tailor the session to you. Arrive a few minutes early so you're not rushed.

What do I wear?

Undress to your comfort level. You're always professionally draped throughout the entire session — only the area being worked on is ever uncovered.

What's your cancellation policy?

Life happens. Just give us a heads up at least 24 hours ahead and we'll happily reschedule you.
