Good to Know

Frequent Questions

Do you take walk-ins?

Yes — walk-ins are always welcome. Booking ahead just guarantees your spot and your barber, especially on weekends.

How do I book?

Use the request form above, call us at (720) 701-8104, or tap the chat button and our booking assistant will set it up in seconds.

Do you do beard work and shaves?

Absolutely. Beard shaping, lineups and classic hot-towel straight-razor shaves are some of our most-requested services.

Can you do custom hair designs?

Yes — freehand designs, parts and patterns are a Motion Kutz specialty. Bring a reference or let your barber freestyle it.

What payment do you take?

Cash and all major cards. Tips are always appreciated, never expected.
