Good to Know

Frequently Asked

Do you take walk-ins?

Walk-ins are always welcome. Booking ahead just guarantees your spot and cuts down your wait.

How do I book an appointment?

Fill out the booking form above with your service and preferred time, or call us at (720) 979-6348. We confirm every booking by phone.

Do you do beard work and shaves?

Absolutely — beard shaping, clean lineups, and classic hot-towel straight-razor shaves are all part of the menu.

What forms of payment do you take?

Cash and all major credit and debit cards. Tips are appreciated but never expected.

Do you cut kids' hair?

Yes. Kids 12 and under get a dedicated cut at a kids' price, with patient barbers who keep it stress-free.
