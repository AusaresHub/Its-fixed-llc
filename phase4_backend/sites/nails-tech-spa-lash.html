Good to Know

Frequently asked

Do you take walk-ins or appointments?
Both. Walk-ins are welcome whenever chairs are open, but booking ahead means no wait and time with your favorite tech. You can book right here in seconds.
How long does gel or dip last?
Typically two to three weeks with proper care, with little to no chipping.
Do you do custom nail art?
Yes — from simple accents to full custom designs. Bring your inspiration photos and we’ll bring it to life.
Do you offer lash services?
Yes. Our lash bar offers extensions to complement your nail appointment — book both in one visit and ask us for the look that suits you.
Are your tools sanitized?
Always. Tools are fully sanitized between every client for your safety and peace of mind.
