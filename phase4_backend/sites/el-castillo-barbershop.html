Good to Know

Questions, Answered

Do you take walk-ins?

Yes — walk-ins are always welcome. Booking ahead just guarantees your spot and saves you the wait when the shop is busy.

How do I book a chair?

Fill out the booking request form on this page, message us using the chat in the corner, or call (720) 983-7063. We'll confirm your time directly.

Do you do beard work and shaves?

Absolutely. We do beard shaping, crisp lineups, and classic hot-towel straight-razor shaves — on their own or paired with a cut.

How much does a cut cost?

Prices start around $35 for a signature haircut and $40 for a skin fade, but they vary by style and length. Call the shop to confirm the price for what you have in mind.

What forms of payment do you take?

We accept cash and all major cards. Tips are always appreciated but never expected.

Do you cut kids' hair?

We do — kids 12 and under get their own patient, no-fuss cut. Bring the whole family.
